Greek Lemon Herb Quinoa Bowl

A light, nutrient-dense Mediterranean bowl packed with fluffy quinoa, crisp cucumbers, kalamata olives, and creamy feta cheese, finished with a bright lemon-herb dressing.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa, cooled
  • 1 cup English cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup pitted kalamata olives, sliced
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • Dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp dried oregano, salt, and pepper

Preparation:

  1. Cook quinoa according to package directions, fluff with a fork, and let cool to room temperature.
  2. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper together in a small ramekin.
  3. In a large bowl, combine the cooled quinoa, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and kalamata olives.
  4. Pour the dressing over the bowl and toss thoroughly to combine.
  5. Top with crumbled feta cheese and serve chilled or at room temperature.
