Honey Chicken

A savory-sweet weeknight dinner made with tender seared chicken breast pieces coated in a sticky honey-garlic glaze and accompanied by crisp vegetables.

Ingredients:

  • 350g boneless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 1 medium broccoli crown, cut into florets
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive or sesame oil

Preparation

  1. Whisk soy sauce, honey, and minced garlic together in a small bowl until fully combined.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add chicken pieces and sear for 5–6 minutes until cooked through. Remove chicken and set aside.
  3. Add broccoli and bell pepper to the same pan; sauté for 3–4 minutes until tender-crisp.
  4. Return the chicken to the pan, pour over the honey-garlic sauce, and simmer for 2 minutes until the sauce thickens and glazes the ingredients.
  5. Serve warm over steamed rice or noodles.
