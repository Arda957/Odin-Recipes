Lasagna Recipe (The Greatest Meal)

A hearty, traditional Italian baked dish layered with rich slow-simmered beef ragù, smooth ricotta filling, tender pasta sheets, and melted golden mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna sheets (oven-ready or pre-boiled)
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 700g marinara or crushed tomato sauce
  • 400g ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 300g shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 50g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt, and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Sauté onion and garlic until soft, then add ground beef and cook until browned. Pour in tomato sauce and oregano, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the grated Parmesan cheese until smooth.
  3. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F).
  4. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Lay down 3–4 lasagna noodles, followed by a layer of ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat layers until ingredients are used, finishing with meat sauce and a generous topping of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  5. Cover loosely with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake uncovered for an additional 15–20 minutes until the top is bubbly and golden brown. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
